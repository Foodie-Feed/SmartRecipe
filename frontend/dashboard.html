Butter Chicken
🏛 Punjab · North India
⏱ Prep: 20 min 🔥 Cook: 35 min 👥 Serves: 4 💚 Health: 72/100
72 Health
Choose a variant:
🧄 Ingredients
  • 500g boneless chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • ½ cup fresh cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek) — a pinch
📋 Method
  1. Marinate chicken with ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, and salt for 30 minutes.
  2. Grill or pan-fry the chicken until lightly charred.
  3. In a pan, melt butter. Add tomato puree and cook for 10 minutes.
  4. Add garam masala, kashmiri chili powder and stir well.
  5. Add grilled chicken into the sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  6. Pour in fresh cream, add kasuri methi and cook for 5 more minutes.
  7. Serve hot with naan or rice.
